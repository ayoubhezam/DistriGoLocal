# 2. Where Distribute Pro is weaker

Part of [Distribute Pro compared with DistriGo](README.md).

Everything below was observed on the phone (Samsung Galaxy A07, 720 × 1600), except where marked "history".

## UX

- **Totals hidden behind the system navigation bar.** On the client page, « Verser client » and « Solde client »
  are drawn under the bar. So are the counters at the bottom of the lists (« Nombre de client », « Total bon »).
- **A dialog taller than the screen.** In « Nouveau client », the « Valider » button is below the fold; the
  seller has to scroll inside the dialog to find it.
- **A dangerous icon in a familiar place.** In « Bons de vente exportés », a one-tap « Supprimer tous les bons »
  icon sits exactly where « + » (new sale) is in the normal sales list.
- **A new sale blocks until a client is picked.** Tapping « Produit » or the tariff first only shows a short
  message. Once a client is picked, a draft note is saved at once; leaving the screen does not offer to discard
  it, so empty « En attente » notes stay in the list.
- **French only, with English leftovers:** « List Clients », « Search », « Password », « GALLERY », « CAMERA ».
  Product names are in Arabic but the interface has no Arabic version; only the printed ticket has Arabic models
  (history).
- **One flat home screen** of five tiles. Every section is a separate screen reached from there, with no way to
  move between sections without going back home.

## Offline

- **Sales work offline, but the phone depends on the server for its data.** Catalogue, prices and stock are only
  as fresh as the last manual sync.
- **Export is manual** and needs the office network (IP) or an FTP server. Nothing is sent in the background.
- **Stock numbers on the phone were not meaningful.** The two products sold stood at −5 and −6, and every other
  product at 0: nothing had been loaded into the van's dépôt, and negative stock is allowed without a warning.

## Workflow

- **No tournée, route or sector**, and no loading or unloading of the van on the phone.
- **No returns, losses or expenses.**
- **Thin reporting:** one statistics screen (sales between two dates, by client) and a stock-state PDF. No
  dashboard, no debt report, no margin report.
- **Purchases, orders, inventory and suppliers exist** (history) but each is a module the installer shows or
  hides, so what a seller can do depends on how the phone was set up.

## Where it is stronger

For balance: Distribute Pro does things DistriGo does not. Its link to the desktop program is the reason it
exists. Colis entry, the remise, free units, tariffs per client and the settings password all match how a
wholesaler's sales rep works. See [the feature table](01-feature-table.md) and
[what DistriGo needs](03-replacement-needs.md).
